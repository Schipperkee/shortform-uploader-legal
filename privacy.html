Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Summary

Shortform Uploader ("the App") is used only by its operator, NoranHood, for their own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone else.

2. What the App handles

  • Account access tokens that the operator grants when connecting their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. These are stored only on the operator's own computer.
  • Basic account info (such as the account's display name and ID) used only to show which account is connected.
  • Videos, titles and descriptions that the operator chooses to upload. They are sent only to the platform the operator selects, and temporary copies are deleted after upload.

3. What the App does not do

The App has no server, no analytics and no advertising. It does not read followers, messages or any other data, and it does not share data with third parties other than the platforms the operator uploads to.

4. Removing access

The operator can disconnect any account in the App, which deletes its stored token, and can also revoke access in each platform's account settings.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be left as an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
