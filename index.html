Shortform Uploader

A personal tool by NoranHood (노란후드)

Shortform Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool that the creator NoranHood uses to upload their own short-form videos to their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts at the same time. It is not offered to the public.
